The pipeline
Six layers of AI,
one seamless result.
0
MediaPipe (Client)

Selfie Segmenter runs entirely in your browser via WebAssembly — no upload needed. It produces a person/background mask that becomes Layer 3 in the dual-mask system, especially helping long hair that drapes over shoulders.

1
BiSeNet Face Parsing

Segments your portrait into 19 semantic regions. Hair is isolated at class 17; eyes, lips, and skin are flagged as protected zones that will never be altered.

2
SAM2 Mask Refinement

Segment Anything Model 2 sharpens the hair boundary using point prompts seeded from the BiSeNet centroid, recovering fine strands and edges BiSeNet misses.

3
LaMa Inpainting

The hair region — plus a dilation zone covering ears, neck, and shoulders hidden beneath long hair — is cleanly erased using Large Mask inpainting, giving SDXL a blank canvas.

4
Groq Vision Analysis

Llama 4 Scout reads your face shape, skin tone, and current length via the Groq API and selects the three styles from Élume's knowledge base that suit you best.

5
SDXL + Face Restore

Stable Diffusion XL renders the new style into the erased region. Your original face pixels are then composited back with a feathered Gaussian blend — identity fully intact.
